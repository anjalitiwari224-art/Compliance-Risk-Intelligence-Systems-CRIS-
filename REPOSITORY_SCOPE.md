# REPOSITORY SCOPE

---

## Overview

The Compliance & Risk Intelligence Systems (CRIS) repository functions as a public infrastructure and methodological framework for:

- regulatory intelligence systems,
- spatial validation methodologies,
- environmental planning coordination,
- workflow intelligence structures,
- and operational built-environment analysis.

The repository documents the conceptual architecture, operational logic, and infrastructure direction of CRIS.

---

## Public Repository Scope

The public repository may include:

- framework documentation,
- methodological systems,
- planning intelligence structures,
- regulatory interpretation workflows,
- environmental coordination methodologies,
- spatial validation systems,
- operational risk frameworks,
- and workflow intelligence architectures.

The repository is intended to support structured infrastructure development and institutional readability.

---

## Infrastructure Position

CRIS is positioned as:

> a modular built-environment intelligence infrastructure framework.

The repository focuses primarily on:

- operational clarity,
- methodological consistency,
- regulatory coordination,
- environmental awareness,
- and scalable planning intelligence systems.

---

## AI Position

Within CRIS, AI is positioned only as an assistive infrastructure layer for:

- regulation parsing,
- workflow organization,
- operational analysis,
- environmental interpretation support,
- and planning coordination assistance.

The repository does not position AI as:

- autonomous approval intelligence,
- legal governance infrastructure,
- engineering replacement,
- or statutory certification systems.

---

## Repository Limitation

The public repository does not necessarily include:

- proprietary implementation systems,
- commercial infrastructure layers,
- deployment architectures,
- cloud coordination systems,
- private workflow engines,
- internal datasets,
- or restricted operational infrastructure.

Certain implementation systems may remain outside the scope of the public repository.

---

## Commercial Boundary

CRIS may evolve into commercial infrastructure systems supporting:

- planning intelligence workflows,
- regulatory coordination platforms,
- environmental analysis systems,
- operational validation tools,
- and workflow intelligence infrastructure.

Commercial systems may be developed separately from the public repository structure.

---

## Research Position

The repository supports exploratory and operational research related to:

- built-environment intelligence systems,
- planning coordination,
- environmental workflows,
- spatial validation methodologies,
- and regulatory interpretation frameworks.

The repository does not function as a statutory authority or approval system.

---

## Institutional Position

CRIS prioritizes:

- institutional readability,
- operational credibility,
- methodological restraint,
- and scalable infrastructure development.

The framework intentionally avoids:

- exaggerated automation narratives,
- speculative technological positioning,
- pseudo-scientific claims,
- and unsupported predictive assertions.

---

## Development Status

The repository remains under active infrastructure and methodological development.

Implementation systems, workflow architectures, visualization infrastructure, and operational intelligence layers may evolve progressively across future development stages.

---

## Final Scope Position

CRIS functions as an evolving infrastructure framework intended to support operational planning intelligence, regulatory coordination, environmental awareness, and structured built-environment analysis workflows.

Final approvals, certifications, legal interpretation, and statutory responsibilities remain with licensed professionals and governing institutions.

# ENVIRONMENTAL SYSTEMS

---

## Overview

The Compliance & Risk Intelligence Systems (CRIS) framework includes an environmental intelligence layer focused on:

- climatic adaptation,
- passive environmental coordination,
- daylight awareness,
- ventilation analysis,
- flood-risk consideration,
- and operational environmental planning systems within built environments.

The framework integrates environmental intelligence into broader planning and compliance workflows.

---

## Environmental Intelligence Position

CRIS treats environmental systems as:

> operational planning infrastructure influencing spatial performance, circulation quality, occupancy conditions, and long-term environmental coordination.

The methodology prioritizes:

- environmental awareness,
- passive planning logic,
- operational adaptability,
- and climate-responsive coordination systems.

## System Limitation

CRIS functions as an AI-assisted environmental planning and operational intelligence support framework.

The system does not:

- replace environmental consultants,
- certify environmental compliance,
- perform engineering-grade simulation,
- or function as a statutory environmental authority.

Final environmental interpretation and approval responsibilities remain with licensed professionals and governing institutions.

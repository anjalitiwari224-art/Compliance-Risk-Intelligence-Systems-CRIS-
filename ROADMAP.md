# CRIS DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP

---

## Overview

The Compliance & Risk Intelligence Systems (CRIS) framework is currently under active methodological and infrastructure development.

The repository presently focuses on:

- planning intelligence systems,
- regulatory interpretation frameworks,
- environmental coordination methodologies,
- spatial validation structures,
- and operational workflow intelligence.

Future implementation systems may evolve progressively across modular infrastructure stages.

## Phase 6 — AI-Assisted Workflow Systems

### Planned Focus

- AI-assisted regulation parsing,
- workflow interpretation support,
- environmental coordination assistance,
- and operational planning analysis systems.

### AI Position

AI within CRIS is intended only for:

- assistive interpretation,
- workflow organization,
- operational analysis,
- and planning coordination support.

AI is not positioned as:

- autonomous approval infrastructure,
- statutory authority,
- engineering certification systems,
- or legal governance intelligence.

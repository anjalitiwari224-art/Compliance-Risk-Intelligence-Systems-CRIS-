# INTEGRATION ARCHITECTURE

---

## Overview

The Compliance & Risk Intelligence Systems (CRIS) framework is intended to support interconnected operational planning-intelligence infrastructure across:

- regulatory systems,
- spatial-validation workflows,
- environmental coordination layers,
- workflow-intelligence systems,
- and operational planning-support architectures.

This document defines the conceptual integration architecture direction of the CRIS framework.

---

# Integration Infrastructure Position

CRIS treats integration systems as:

> operational coordination infrastructure enabling structured interoperability between planning-intelligence layers.

The framework prioritizes:

- modular interoperability,
- workflow traceability,
- operational readability,
- and scalable infrastructure coordination.

The objective is to support interconnected planning systems rather than isolated intelligence modules.

---

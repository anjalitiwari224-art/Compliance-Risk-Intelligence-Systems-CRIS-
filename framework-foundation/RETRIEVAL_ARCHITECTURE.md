# RETRIEVAL ARCHITECTURE

---

## Overview

The Compliance & Risk Intelligence Systems (CRIS) framework is intended to support structured retrieval and interpretation workflows across:

- regulatory systems,
- spatial validation systems,
- environmental planning systems,
- workflow coordination structures,
- and operational risk-analysis layers.

This document defines the conceptual retrieval architecture direction of the CRIS framework.

---

# Retrieval Infrastructure Position

CRIS treats retrieval systems as:

> operational planning intelligence coordination infrastructure.

The objective is to support:

- structured interpretation,
- workflow visibility,
- operational traceability,
- and scalable planning intelligence systems.

The framework prioritizes interpretable retrieval workflows rather than opaque automation systems.

---

# Primary Retrieval Categories

Future retrieval systems may organize information across categories including:

1. Regulatory Retrieval
2. Spatial Retrieval
3. Environmental Retrieval
4. Workflow Retrieval
5. Risk-Analysis Retrieval
6. Cross-System Retrieval
7. Operational Metadata Retrieval

Each retrieval layer functions within broader planning intelligence workflows.

# Retrieval Modularity Principle

All retrieval systems within CRIS are intended to remain:

- modular,
- independently scalable,
- operationally interpretable,
- and adaptable across jurisdictions and project types.

This allows future infrastructure expansion without destabilizing foundational system architecture.

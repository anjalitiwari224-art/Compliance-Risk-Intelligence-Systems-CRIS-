# CODE OF CONDUCT

---

## Overview

The Compliance & Risk Intelligence Systems (CRIS) repository is intended to support professional, respectful, and operationally focused collaboration.

All contributors, reviewers, and participants are expected to maintain constructive and institutionally appropriate conduct.

---

## Expected Conduct

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully,
- support structured discussion,
- maintain professional collaboration standards,
- and prioritize operational clarity.

---

## Unacceptable Conduct

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- harassment,
- discriminatory language,
- abusive communication,
- disruptive behavior,
- intentional misinformation,
- and bad-faith collaboration practices.

---

## Repository Position

CRIS prioritizes:

- institutional credibility,
- operational professionalism,
- structured methodology,
- and responsible planning intelligence development.

---

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers reserve the right to moderate, restrict, or remove contributions that violate repository conduct standards.

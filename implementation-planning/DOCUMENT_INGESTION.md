# DOCUMENT INGESTION

---

## Overview

The Compliance & Risk Intelligence Systems (CRIS) framework is intended to support structured document-ingestion workflows across:

- regulatory systems,
- spatial-validation infrastructure,
- environmental planning systems,
- workflow-intelligence layers,
- and operational planning-support architectures.

This document defines the conceptual document-ingestion architecture direction for future CRIS infrastructure development.

---

# Ingestion Infrastructure Position

CRIS treats document ingestion as:

> operational planning-intelligence intake infrastructure.

The framework prioritizes:

- structured traceability,
- semantic readability,
- operational organization,
- modular scalability,
- and workflow coordination visibility.

The objective is to transform planning-related documentation into interpretable operational intelligence systems.

---

# Primary Ingestion Categories

Future ingestion systems may support:

1. Regulatory Documents
2. Zoning & Bylaw Systems
3. Environmental Planning Documents
4. Workflow & Approval Records
5. Spatial Coordination Documents

Each category functions within broader planning-intelligence workflows.

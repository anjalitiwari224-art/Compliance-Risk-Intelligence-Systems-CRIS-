# REGULATORY SCHEMA

---

## Overview

The Compliance & Risk Intelligence Systems (CRIS) framework is intended to support structured regulatory-intelligence workflows across built-environment planning systems.

This document defines the preliminary conceptual schema direction for regulatory infrastructure within CRIS.

The objective is to support:

- structured regulatory interpretation,
- semantic retrieval,
- workflow coordination,
- operational traceability,
- and planning-intelligence interoperability.

---

# Regulatory Infrastructure Position

CRIS treats regulatory systems as:

> structured operational planning-intelligence reference infrastructure.

The framework prioritizes:

- traceability,
- semantic readability,
- workflow interoperability,
- modular scalability,
- and structured regulatory coordination.

The schema is intended to support interpretable planning workflows rather than autonomous legal infrastructure.

---

- independently scalable,
- operationally interpretable,
- and adaptable across jurisdictions and project types.

This allows future infrastructure expansion without destabilizing foundational methodology.

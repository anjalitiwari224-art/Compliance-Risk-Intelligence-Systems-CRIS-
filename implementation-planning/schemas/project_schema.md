# PROJECT SCHEMA

---

## Overview

The Compliance & Risk Intelligence Systems (CRIS) framework is intended to support operational planning-intelligence workflows centered around structured project coordination systems.

This document defines the preliminary conceptual project-schema direction for future CRIS infrastructure development.

The objective is to support:

- project-level coordination,
- regulatory traceability,
- workflow interoperability,
- environmental planning awareness,
- spatial-validation systems,
- and operational planning-intelligence integration.

---

# Project Infrastructure Position

CRIS treats projects as:

> operational planning-intelligence coordination environments.

The framework prioritizes:

- workflow traceability,
- semantic readability,
- regulatory coordination,
- operational interoperability,
- and scalable planning-intelligence infrastructure.

Project systems are intended to support interpretable operational workflows rather than autonomous governance infrastructure.

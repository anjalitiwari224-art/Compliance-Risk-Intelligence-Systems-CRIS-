# WORKFLOW SCHEMA

---

## Overview

The Compliance & Risk Intelligence Systems (CRIS) framework is intended to support structured workflow-intelligence infrastructure across:

- regulatory systems,
- approval coordination,
- spatial-validation workflows,
- environmental planning systems,
- and operational planning-support architectures.

This document defines the preliminary conceptual workflow-schema direction for future CRIS infrastructure development.

The objective is to support:

- workflow traceability,
- operational coordination,
- approval sequencing,
- dependency management,
- and planning-intelligence interoperability.

---

# Workflow Infrastructure Position

CRIS treats workflows as:

> operational planning-coordination systems.

The framework prioritizes:

- structured sequencing,
- operational visibility,
- dependency traceability,
- workflow readability,
- and scalable coordination infrastructure.

The schema is intended to support interpretable operational systems rather than autonomous approval infrastructure.

---

# SEMANTIC TAGGING

---

## Overview

The Compliance & Risk Intelligence Systems (CRIS) framework is intended to support structured semantic-tagging workflows across:

- regulatory systems,
- spatial-validation infrastructure,
- environmental planning systems,
- workflow-intelligence layers,
- and operational planning-support architectures.

This document defines the conceptual semantic-tagging architecture direction for future CRIS infrastructure development.

---

# Semantic Infrastructure Position

CRIS treats semantic tagging as:

> operational planning-intelligence classification infrastructure.

The framework prioritizes:

- semantic traceability,
- operational readability,
- structured interpretation,
- workflow coordination visibility,
- and scalable planning-intelligence organization.

The objective is to support interpretable semantic coordination systems rather than opaque automation architectures.

---

# Core Semantic Categories

Future semantic systems may support tagging across:

1. Regulatory Tags

4. Workflow Tags

# CONTRIBUTING

---

## Overview

The Compliance & Risk Intelligence Systems (CRIS) repository is currently under active methodological and infrastructure development.

Contributions should prioritize:

- operational clarity,
- institutional credibility,
- methodological consistency,
- and structured planning intelligence systems.

---

## Contribution Scope

Contributions may include:

- regulatory intelligence structuring,
- environmental planning workflows,
- spatial validation systems,
- operational coordination methodologies,
- workflow analysis structures,
- and documentation refinement.

---

## Contribution Standards

All contributions should:

- maintain methodological consistency,
- avoid speculative technological claims,
- avoid pseudo-scientific positioning,
- prioritize operational readability,
- and support scalable planning intelligence systems.

---

## AI & Automation Position

CRIS is positioned as an AI-assisted planning intelligence framework.

Contributions should not represent the system as:

- autonomous approval infrastructure,
- legal certification authority,
- engineering replacement,
- or statutory governance intelligence.

---

## Repository Conduct

Contributors are expected to maintain:

- professional communication,
- operational clarity,
- institutional respect,
- and structured collaboration standards.

---

## Development Position

The repository remains under active infrastructure and methodological development.

Certain implementation systems, automation layers, and commercial infrastructure components may remain outside the scope of the public repository.
